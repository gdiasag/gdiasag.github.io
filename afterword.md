This website started as a fork of [Eliza Weisman]'s personal page [elizas.website], and most of its stylistic choices are derived from it, also followed by modifications from [Peter Coles]' [`markdown-css`] and [Pascal Herleif]'s [`jekyll-theme-scribbles`].

Currently typeset in [Commit Mono] by [Eigil Nikolajsen] and themed with the `:colorscheme` of your preference.
